# Concept And Message House Guide

Use this guide after Level 4 has selected an Idea Platform. In a complete run,
Concept Package is required even when the strategy remains Provisional. Skip it
only when the user explicitly stops at an earlier stage or Input Readiness is
Unavailable.

The Idea Platform is the strategic roof for the whole brand or campaign period.
A Concept is a specific expression route under that platform. Each Concept uses
a Message House to keep its claim, support, and proof coherent.

## Relationship

```text
Idea Platform
  -> Concept A -> Message House -> executions
  -> Concept B -> Message House -> executions
  -> Concept C -> Message House -> executions
```

- **Idea Platform**: one strategic platform; answers what the brand should
  stand for and prove.
- **Concept**: a concrete expression territory; answers what this campaign,
  content series, activation, product experience, or touchpoint should say/do.
- **Message House**: the message architecture for one Concept; keeps the claim
  focused and evidence-backed.

Do not let a Concept replace the Idea Platform. Concepts are plural and
execution-facing. The Idea Platform is singular and strategy-facing.

## Message House Structure

For each Concept:

- **Roof**: the single core message or claim of this Concept, with Evidence IDs
  showing the human, cultural, and brand basis.
- **Pillars**: exactly 2-3 supporting messages that explain the Roof, each with
  Evidence IDs.
- **Foundation**: concrete product facts, service facts, brand behaviors,
  demonstrations, or sourced observations. Record proof type, exact fact,
  Evidence IDs, and confirmation status for every item.

If the roof cannot be supported by pillars and foundation, the Concept is weak.
If the pillars do not clearly support the roof, the Message House is unfocused.
If the Foundation is mostly adjectives rather than proof, it is invalid. Revise
it or record the missing proof as a gap; never use adjectives such as
"professional", "gentle", or "trustworthy" as standalone Foundation items.

## Derivation Steps

1. Start from the selected Idea Platform Record.
2. List possible Concept angles: product, content, experience, event, channel,
   community, partnership, retail, service, or social conversation.
3. For each angle, write one Roof claim.
4. Add 2-3 Pillars that explain or dramatize the Roof.
5. Add Foundation proof from brand facts, product proof, service proof, brand
   behavior, or evidence pool items. Attach Evidence IDs.
6. Complete one `assets/templates/concept-record-template.md` per Concept.
7. Evaluate concepts for relevance, distinction, credibility, action potential,
   and fit with the Idea Platform.
8. Choose one recommended Concept and 1-2 alternatives. Explain why each
   alternative was retained and what would make it preferable.
9. Assemble `assets/templates/concept-package-template.md` and write it into the
   dossier's `## 8. Concept Records` section.

For every retained Concept, keep these controller-facing fields explicit:
`Concept name`, `One-line concept`, `Audience role`, `Expression territory`,
`Risk`, and `Confidence`. Do not expect the controller to infer them from longer
articulation paragraphs.

## Mini Example

Selected Idea Platform:

```text
Because urban skincare users feel caught between self-optimization pressure and
the desire to feel already enough, the brand should stand for returning skincare
to self-care rather than self-assessment, and prove it through simpler formulas
and non-pressure communication behavior.
```

| Concept | Roof | Angle | Pillars | Foundation facts | Evidence IDs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product concept | Fewer ingredients, easier trust | Product | simpler formula; lower decision stress; daily usability | 9-item formula; repeat-purchase comments tied to ease | BF-001, E3, E10 |
| Communication concept | We do not hurry you into becoming better | Content | no fear-based comparison; honest product limits | no before/after image; service declines anti-aging overclaim | BF-002, BF-003, E7, E12 |
| Experience concept | Night skincare as rest | Occasion | evening care; less self-judgment | supplied night-use proposition and occasion evidence | BF-004, E6, E8 |

## Concept Quality Checks

- Does it clearly serve the selected Idea Platform?
- Does it have one Roof, not several competing claims?
- Do the Pillars explain the Roof rather than introduce unrelated topics?
- Does the Foundation contain proof, not just tone words?
- Does every Roof, Pillar, and Foundation item carry a traceable Evidence ID?
- Is it meaningfully different from other Concepts under the same platform?
- Can it produce executions across the intended channels?
- Are proof gaps and risks explicit?
- Does Final / Provisional status match the weakest critical dependency?

## Status Rules

- `Final`: the selected Idea Platform is Final; the Concept's Roof and Pillars
  are supported; each critical Foundation item is confirmed or credibly
  reported; no unresolved gap undermines the core promise.
- `Provisional`: a useful Concept can be formed, but the Idea Platform or one or
  more critical proofs, behaviors, or competitor distinctions still need
  confirmation.
- `Reject`: the Concept lacks fit, distinction, or credible proof. Do not place
  rejected Concepts in the final alternatives list.

A complete Message House does not automatically qualify as Final.

## Output

Use one `concept-record-template.md` per Concept. Multiple Concept Records can
share the same `Idea Platform source`, but their Roof claims must be distinct.
Package the recommendation and alternatives with evidence support, proof gaps,
risks, and overall status. Do not continue into campaign copy, scripts, visual
design, or execution assets.
