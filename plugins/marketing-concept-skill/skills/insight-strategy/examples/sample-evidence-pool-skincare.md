# Evidence Pool

> Development-only `Evidence Pool v1` worked sample for a fictional Chinese
> skincare brand, 屿白 YUBAI. Do not load it during live strategy work.

Handoff preservation: preserve Evidence ID and all core field names and values.
Keep Observation even when Raw quote is unavailable.

## Source Scope

- Research question: 在功效内卷的护肤市场里，年轻消费者为什么会被「不强调抗老/逆龄」的品牌吸引，又在担心什么？
- Brand / category: 屿白 YUBAI / 面部护肤（基础修护、保湿）
- Market: 中国大陆，一线及新一线城市，小红书+天猫为主
- Date range: 2026-03-01 ~ 2026-06-10
- Included source types: social, review, competitor, interview, survey, user provided
- Excluded source types: 付费水军评论、与护肤无关的泛生活内容
- Known limitations: 样本偏小红书女性用户，男性与下沉市场声音不足；天猫评价含一定营销返现影响
