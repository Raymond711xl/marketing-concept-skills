### Recommended Concept C-01

- Concept name: Tonight, skincare is not homework
- One-line concept: Turn night skincare from a second shift of self-assessment into permission to stop.
- Audience role: A person allowed to receive care without completing another optimization task.
- Expression territory: Night-time release, basic care, and non-pressuring brand behavior.
- Confidence: medium
- Roof: Tonight, skincare is not homework. Evidence IDs: WEB-002, WEB-006, WEB-008.
- Pillar 1: basic care can be enough. Evidence IDs: WEB-003, WEB-010, BF-001.
- Pillar 2: the brand does not sell through inadequacy. Evidence IDs: WEB-001, WEB-007,
  WEB-012, BF-002, BF-003.
- Foundation: 9-item formula (BF-001); no before/after imagery (WEB-007, BF-003);
  non-overpromising service reply (WEB-012, BF-002).
- Proof gaps: cross-channel consistency and founder belief.
- Risk: anti-anxiety language may be read as another sales device (WEB-011).
- Concept status: Provisional.

### Alternative Concept C-02

- Concept name: Taking care of the basics
- One-line concept: Replace complex skincare homework with fewer, clearer acts of basic care.
- Audience role: A daily user making a simple choice rather than managing a research project.
- Expression territory: Minimal product facts, basic efficacy, and lower decision burden.
- Confidence: medium
- Roof: Taking care of the basics is already effective. Evidence IDs: WEB-003,
  WEB-010, WEB-012, BF-001.
- Pillars: fewer stacking decisions (WEB-003, WEB-010); restrained claims (WEB-012,
  BF-002).
- Foundation: formula list (BF-001); repeat-purchase review (WEB-010); service
  script (WEB-012, BF-002).
- Proof gaps: competitor comparison and product-result boundaries.
- Risk: may collapse into a generic minimal-formula proposition.
- Concept status: Provisional.
